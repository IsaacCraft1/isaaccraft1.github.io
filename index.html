How to Play Table Tennis

Core Rules of the Game

  • The Service: The ball must bounce once on your side, clear the net, and bounce on the opponent's side.
  • Scoring Points: Games are played up to 11 points.
  • Alternating Service: Service alternates every 2 points.

Why Table Tennis is Beneficial

Physical & Mental Advantages

  • Cardiovascular Health: Spikes your heart rate and burns calories efficiently.
  • Hand-Eye Coordination: Sharpens control and spatial tracking capabilities.

History of Table Tennis

Click a dot to reveal details

1880s

1880s: Victorian Origins

Table tennis began as a makeshift upper class parlor game in England using rolled up books as nets and cigar box lids as bats.

1901

1901: The Ping Pong Name

Celluloid balls were introduced. The sound of the ball hitting wooden paddles led to the trademarked name Ping Pong.

1926

1926: Official Organization

The International Table Tennis Federation was formed and held the first World Championships in London.

1950s

1950s: The Sponge Revolution

Sponge rubber rackets were introduced, creating high speeds and heavy spin that changed how the game was played globally.

1988

1988: Olympic Status

Table tennis made its debut as an official Olympic sport at the Seoul Games in South Korea.

2000s

2000s: Modern Rule Changes

The ITTF increased ball size from 38mm to 40mm to slow down play for television audiences, and lowered winning game points from 21 down to 11.
